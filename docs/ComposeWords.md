# Compose New Words

Build a Bliss word that is not in the vocabulary, name it, use it in a message, and save it to a palette.

## Build a word

1. Select **Compose new word** on the command bar. A dashed box marked "New word" opens in the message, at the caret.
2. Add words and characters from any palette or from "Add Symbol to Message". They go into the box.
3. Edit the word with the usual commands: Backward, Forward, Delete, Delete all, add modifier and
   undo modifier.
4. Select **Done** to name the word, or **Cancel** to drop it.

While a word is being built, "add indicator" and "remove indicator" are unavailable. Add an indicator to the
finished word instead: it goes on the word's classifier. Speak and Make Sentences come back after Done or Cancel.

## The building controls

| Control | What it does |
| ------- | ------------ |
| Split | Breaks the part at the caret into its characters. Teacher becomes person, giving and knowledge. With the combine marker on, a comma goes between them. |
| Combine marker | Puts a combine marker at each end of the word, and a comma between the words already there. Select it again to take both markers and those commas away. |
| Space | Adds a space after the caret. |
| Comma | Adds a comma after the caret. |
| Cancel | Drops the word and goes back to the message. |
| Done | Finishes the word, then asks for its name. |

## Commas and spaces

1. Turning the combine marker on puts a comma between every two neighbouring words already there.
2. Once the marker is on, each word added gets a comma between it and the word before it, and Split puts a comma
   between the characters it makes.
3. Turning the marker off removes those commas. Commas and spaces added with Comma and Space stay.
4. The caret stops on a comma or a space. Delete removes it. Comma and Space add one.
5. Deleting a word also deletes the comma or space before it.
6. Comma and Space work without the marker too.

In the finished word, a comma sits a quarter space from its neighbours, like the characters. A space is a full space.

## Name the word

After Done, the dialog offers the parts' names joined, marked "(default)", for example "woman giving
knowledge". Select it, or "Use default name and close", to use it. Pictures add no name, and when no
part has a name, no default is offered. A helper can type another name and
select "Use this name". On the desktop version, the dialog also asks the AI model for names as it
opens, showing "Asking the AI model for names…" and a placeholder for each name until they arrive.
They are marked as AI suggestions.

When the word is already in the vocabulary, the dialog says so, for example "This is teacher", and offers
that word.

Grammatical indicators, such as action and plural, are dropped from the parts. The thing and abstract
indicators stay.

## Save the word to a palette

Go to the palette the word belongs on, put the caret on the word and select **Save new word**. The word
is added at the end of the palette, in a new row when the palette is full.
A word can be saved only until a modifier or indicator is added to it in the message; after that it is
an ordinary edited word. Once saved to a palette, it cannot be saved to that palette again.

On the desktop version, saved words are kept on this computer. On the public website, they are gone
after a reload. "Clear all saved data" removes them.
